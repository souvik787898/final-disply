SOUVIK BISWAS

My practice is connected to my surroundings, nature, and childhood memories. I believe that if we observe nature closely, we can find reflections of our own emotions, memories, and daily lives within it. I choose the unseen as the subject of my work because I want it to be seen, understood, and valued. Through my paintings, I explore the relationship between humans and nature, encouraging viewers to appreciate the beauty of the natural world in its own unique way.

Insects and other small living beings play an important role in my work because they are often ignored, even though they are essential to the ecosystem. They represent survival, balance, and the hidden life that exists all around us. Through my art, I invite people to look more carefully at nature and become more sensitive to the lives that usually go unnoticed.

I work with different mediums and techniques, including charcoal, acrylic, synthetic additives, binders, and mixed media. Along with traditional materials, I also use technology and interactive elements to create a deeper connection between the artwork and the viewer. My aim is to encourage viewers not only to see the work but also to experience it, connect with it emotionally, and feel its essence through interaction.

By observing my surroundings and reconnecting with nature, I transform my childhood memories and personal experiences into visual forms, creating works that invite reflection, curiosity, and a deeper understanding of our relationship with the natural world.
