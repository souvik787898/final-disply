About

Souvik Biswas (b. 1996, Deganga, West Bengal) is a visual art student based in Kolkata. His practice explores the relationship between nature, memory, and human experience through painting, sculpture, and mixed media. Inspired by childhood memories and close observation of insects, plants, and other often-overlooked forms of life, his work highlights the hidden beauty and ecological importance of the natural world. By combining traditional materials with experimental and interactive approaches, he creates artworks that invite viewers to slow down, observe, and reconnect with nature. Souvik is currently pursuing a Master of Fine Arts in Painting at Rabindra Bharati University. His work has been exhibited at the Academy of Fine Arts, Birla Academy of Art & Culture, Jahangir Art Gallery, and other national exhibitions and group shows across India.

Content

My practice is connected to my surroundings, nature, and childhood memories. I believe that if we observe nature closely, we can find reflections of our own emotions, memories, and daily lives within it. I choose the unseen as the subject of my work because I want it to be seen, understood, and valued. Through my paintings, I explore the relationship between humans and nature, encouraging viewers to appreciate the beauty of the natural world in its own unique way.

Insects and other small living beings play an important role in my work because they are often ignored, even though they are essential to the ecosystem. They represent survival, balance, and the hidden life that exists all around us. Through my art, I invite people to look more carefully at nature and become more sensitive to the lives that usually go unnoticed. I work with different mediums and techniques, including charcoal, acrylic, synthetic additives, binders, and mixed media. Along with traditional materials, I also use technology and interactive elements to create a deeper connection between the artwork and the viewer. My aim is to encourage viewers not only to see the work but also to experience it, connect with it emotionally, and feel its essence through interaction.

By observing my surroundings and reconnecting with nature, I transform my childhood memories and personal experiences into visual forms, creating works that invite reflection, curiosity, and a deeper understanding of our relationship with the natural world.

Biography

Souvik Biswas is an Indian visual art student whose practice explores the relationship between nature, memory, and the unseen world. Working with painting, printmaking, mixed media, and interactive installations, he investigates insects, fungi, and overlooked living organisms as metaphors for survival, transformation, and interconnectedness.
