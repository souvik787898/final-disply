Souvik Biswas

Artist | Painter | Printmaker

Artist Statement

My practice is connected to my surroundings, nature, and childhood memories. I believe that if we observe nature closely, we can find reflections of our own emotions, memories, and daily lives within it.

Through my paintings I explore the relationship between humans and nature, encouraging viewers to appreciate the beauty of the natural world in its own unique way.

I work with different mediums and techniques including charcoal, acrylic, mixed media and printmaking to create immersive visual experiences.
